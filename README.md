# seq2song
An RNN model to predict the next character of sheet music in ABC notation, to generate a brand new music file.

The experience was bittersweet. As a professional musician myself, I was in awe of how effective these models remain; at the same time, I felt a real tension, because even to a seasoned listener, the generated piece can sound indistinguishable from the original.

That raises questions I’m still sitting with:
- If an audience cannot tell, what exactly are we valuing: the signal itself, or the intention behind it?
- Where does “feel” live: in micro-timing, dynamics, phrasing, or in the story of the person who plays the notes?
- When a model recombines statistical patterns to produce something plausible, is it a co-composer, or simply a mirror held up to our corpus?
- I don’t think these questions diminish the technology; they deepen my respect for both craft and code.
